# Design System: Clinical Precision

## Brand & Style
This design system is anchored in the concept of "Clinical Precision"—a blend of high-end hospitality and medical expertise. The aesthetic is strictly minimalist, leaning into a Corporate/Modern style that prioritizes clarity, hygiene, and calm.

## Style Guidelines
- **Margins:** A consistent 24px side margin ensures content does not feel cramped against the bezel.
- **Rhythm:** An 8px linear scale (with 4px half-steps) governs all vertical spacing.
- **Interactive Density:** Every interactive element adheres to a minimum 44px touch target.
- **Dental Diagram:** The 32-tooth diagram follows a 16x2 grid arrangement.

## Colors
- **Primary:** #0F172A (Deep Navy)
- **Secondary:** #0D9488 (Muted Teal)
- **Background:** #F7F9FB
- **Surface:** #F7F9FB
- **Error:** #BA1A1A

## Typography
- **Headlines:** Manrope (600 weight)
- **Body:** Inter (400 weight)
- **Labels:** Inter (600 weight, caps)

## Shapes
- **Buttons:** 4px radius
- **Cards:** 8px radius
- **Tooth Diagram:** 2px radius
